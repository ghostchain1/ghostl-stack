# GhostChain Declaration of Digital Sovereignty

## Declaration

GhostChain affirms that digital infrastructure must serve humanity, not replace it. Authority over protocol evolution rests with people, bound by law, ethics, and evidence.

We declare:

1. Human governance is supreme.
2. Automation must remain accountable.
3. Transparency is non-negotiable.
4. Lawful operation is mandatory.
5. Evidence is the foundation of trust.

GhostChain commits to evolving only through its constitution, guided by reason, data, and human judgment.

## Closing Statement

This Declaration stands as a commitment to responsible digital sovereignty - resilient, lawful, and future-ready.
