**① Gas Volatility**
> Gas prices spike 10,000%+ during demand events. Users are priced out. Applications break. This is not a market feature — it is an architectural failure.

**② Fragmented Liquidity**
> $2.5B+ lost to bridge exploits. 0.3–2% additional slippage per transaction from fragmentation. Hundreds of isolated chains with no constitutional coordination.

> Token-weighted governance is gameable by large holders. Voter apathy leaves protocols ungoverned. No constitutional invariants prevent governance attacks.

> Validators are rewarded for block production regardless of service quality. Slashing is narrow and difficult to enforce. Cartels form without constitutional consequence.

> Validators run at full capacity regardless of load. Batching is suboptimal. Gas targets are set conservatively, not optimally. Energy per transaction is not minimized.
